Ebb Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Ebb is built to work almost entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what health data exists, where it lives, and who sees it.

What we store, and where

Your daily symptom logs, HRT dose history, cycle data, and any notes you write are stored locally on your device (and shared between the Ebb app and its widgets via an iOS App Group container). This data is not uploaded to any server we operate. We do not have a backend, and we cannot see your health data.

Doctor visit reports are generated entirely on your device as a PDF, which you choose to share via iOS's standard Share Sheet. We never see or receive a copy.

Notifications (daily check-in reminders, HRT dose reminders) are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is involved.

Purchases

Ebb offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple and by our subscription infrastructure provider, RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a device/app-scoped identifier and purchase/transaction data necessary to validate your subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details. We do not receive or store your payment details — Apple handles all billing.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad tracking.
  • No account creation, no email collection.
  • No selling or sharing of your health data with third parties for marketing.
  • No analytics SDKs beyond the RevenueCat purchase-validation data described above.

Your control

Because your health data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes that data. You can remove specific entries directly in the app at any time.

Medical disclaimer

Ebb is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, HRT dosing, or any treatment decision.
